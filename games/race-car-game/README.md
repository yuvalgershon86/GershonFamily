# 🏁 Race City

A car game! Open `index.html` in a web browser to play.

- **🏎️ Races** – race 3 rival cars on 4 tracks and win gold, silver and bronze trophies.
- **🚕 Taxi** – pick up the waving people and drive them to the flag before your shift ends.
- **🚗 Free Drive** – cruise around the city and collect coins.
- **🎨 Garage** – paint your car any color, add stickers (flames, stars, checkers…), buy new cars and upgrade your engine, tires and nitro.
- **🏆 Trophies** – see all the cups and awards you've won.

Controls: arrow keys or W A S D to drive, Space for nitro. On a tablet or phone, use the buttons on the screen.
Your money, car and trophies are saved in the browser.
